## How this portfolio is organised

- **Collect:** Keep the original artefacts, screenshots, documents and records produced during the work.
- **Select:** Include relevant artefacts and explain why each one is appropriate evidence.
- **Reflect:** Explain what the artefact demonstrates, what was learned and what could be improved.
- **Connect:** Show how the charter, stories, backlogs and logbook relate to one another.

## Evidence integrity

This is an individual assessment. The content must describe my own work and actual project activity. Any item marked **[TO ADD]** or **[TO VERIFY]** is a prompt for me to complete using my own records. No meeting, sprint result, task completion, team decision or screenshot should be claimed unless it actually happened and can be supported.
