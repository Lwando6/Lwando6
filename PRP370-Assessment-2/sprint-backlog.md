Suitable evidence includes:
- Screenshot of the sprint board at planning and/or completion.
- Sprint goal and selected stories.
- Task assignments or estimates.
- Dated progress updates.
- Demonstration, test result or completed work artefact.
- Notes showing blockers and how they were addressed.

1. Was the sprint goal clear and achievable?
2. Did the selected work align with the product backlog priorities?
3. Were tasks sufficiently small and clear?
4. What helped or prevented progress?
5. What would you do differently when planning the next sprint?

Use specific tasks, dates and evidence. Do not report a sprint outcome that cannot be verified.
