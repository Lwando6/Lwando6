## 3. How to capture the remaining evidence

1. Open the original CareerCraft Trello workspace and board.
2. Capture the board overview showing its name and lists.
3. Open relevant cards and capture the description, members, due date, checklist and activity where available.
4. Export the board if your Trello workspace and permissions provide that option.
5. Preserve original dates and card details. Do not recreate old cards and label them as historical.
6. Upload screenshots or exports into this repository's `evidence/` folder.
7. Link each uploaded file in the relevant Sprint Backlog and Agile Logbook section.

If only part of the original board is accessible, state which lists/cards are available and which historical periods are missing.

## 4. Evidence integrity

- A screenshot proves only what is visible in that screenshot.
- A card's current status does not prove when it entered that status unless activity history or dated export supports it.
- A Trello card does not automatically prove that its task was completed or tested.
- A reconstructed board created now must be labelled as reconstructed from the source logbook and repository history.
- Do not expose private team information, access tokens or unrelated personal data in public screenshots.
