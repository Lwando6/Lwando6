## 1. Purpose

An Agile logbook records project activity over time. The assessment brief describes it as a way to record daily stand-ups, sprint plans and retrospective notes.

Use dated entries. If an activity did not take place, do not create a fictional entry. For an individual project, record your actual progress, planning, review and reflection.
